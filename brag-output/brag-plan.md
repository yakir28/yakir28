# /brag — Shopify (9:16 SaaS showcase ad)

**Input:** shopify.com (website). The live site was blocked by this environment's network policy, so the copy and identity come from Shopify's long-standing public positioning instead: "From first sale to full scale", "Start for free", brand greens `#95BF47` / `#008060`, and a dark/cream editorial palette. No customer logos, testimonials or platform-wide stats are invented. The numbers inside the demo store UI are illustrative.

**Format:** vertical 1080×1920, 30fps, 20.5s.

## Answers
- **What is it?** The commerce platform that lets anyone build a store, sell everywhere and run the business from one place.
- **Who is it for?** Anyone who wants to start selling, from first-time founders to brands that are already scaling.
- **What sets it apart?** One platform covers the storefront, every sales channel, payments, shipping and analytics.
- **Hook:** the "ka-ching": order notifications piling up on a lockscreen. Every merchant's dream, and the sound everyone associates with Shopify.
- **Tone:** `default`: punchy, playful, clean, with app-store-style feature beats.
- **Share caption:** "Ka-ching. That's the sound of your next sale."

## Storyboard (beats at 120 BPM, cuts on the bar)
| # | Time | Scene | On screen |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0.0–3.0 | **Hook** | Black. "Ka-ching." slams in, in green. A phone lockscreen fills with "New order" notifications, each one with a bell. Line: "That's the sound of your next sale." |
| 2 | 3.0–6.0 | **Reveal** | A green circle wipes out of the phone into cream. Bag mark + "shopify" wordmark. "From first sale to full scale." The beat drops. |
| 3 | 6.0–10.0 | **Build** | "Build your store." A demo store (Bloom & Brew coffee) assembles block by block: nav, hero, products. The cursor clicks Add to cart, the badge pops and an "Added to cart" toast appears. |
| 4 | 10.0–14.0 | **Sell everywhere** | Dark green. "Sell everywhere." The store sits at the hub. Lines draw out to Online store, Social media, In person and Marketplaces, and orders flow back in as pulses. "One store. Every channel." |
| 5 | 14.0–17.0 | **Grow** | Cream. "Watch it grow." A dashboard with the sales counter ticking up, a line chart drawing and rows for paid, label printed and fulfilled. "Payments, shipping and analytics, built in." |
| 6 | 17.0–20.5 | **CTA** | Green. "Start your business." The cursor clicks a white "Start for free" button. shopify.com. Final chord. |

## Sound
An original synthesized 120 BPM house-pop loop in A minor/C (Am–F–C–G). The hook is kick plus bell "ka-chings" tuned to the key (E6/A6). The full beat drops at the reveal. Filtered-noise whooshes mark the wipes, soft in-key plucks the pops and the click, and a sustained chord closes the end card.
